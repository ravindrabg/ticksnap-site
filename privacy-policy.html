TickSnap Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026 · App: TickSnap (com.mgudde.rbg.ticksnap) · Developer: rbg

TickSnap is an interval camera for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: your photos stay on your phone, there is no account and there are no ads.

Photos you take

Photos are saved in TickSnap's private storage on your phone. If you turn on Also copy to phone Gallery or tap To Gallery, copies are saved in your phone's Pictures folder. We never receive your photos. They leave your phone only when you share them yourself through Android's share menu, to the app or person you choose. Password-protected ZIP passwords are never stored by the app.

Camera

TickSnap uses the camera to show the preview and take photos, including in the background while an interval loop you started is running (with a notification).

Face and hand detection

TickSnap detects faces (to show where they are) and hands (so you can wave or hold up your palm to take a photo). This uses Google ML Kit and Google MediaPipe models that run entirely on your phone. Camera images are not sent to us or to Google for this.

Data collected by Google ML Kit

Google ML Kit, which TickSnap uses for face detection, sends Google technical information about how the feature performs. According to Google's ML Kit disclosure, this includes:

DataExamplesPurpose
Device informationManufacturer, model, Android versionDiagnostics and usage analytics
App informationPackage name, app versionDiagnostics and usage analytics
Device or other identifiersA device identifier and per-installation identifiersDiagnostics and usage analytics
Performance and usage of the featureLatency, image format and size, feature events, error codesDiagnostics and usage analytics

Google encrypts this data in transit and says it does not transfer it to third parties. It does not include your photos or the faces detected in them. Google's handling is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the ML Kit terms.

App updates

When TickSnap starts, it asks the Google Play Store app on your phone whether a newer version is available. This check is handled by Google Play.

What we don't do

  • We don't run servers, collect analytics of our own, or show ads.
  • We don't access your location, contacts, microphone or other apps' files.
  • We don't sell or share personal information.

Children

TickSnap is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete loops or photos inside the app, or uninstall TickSnap to remove everything it stored. Copies you saved to your Gallery or shared stay where you put them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
