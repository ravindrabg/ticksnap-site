TickSnap Privacy Policy

Effective 26 November 2026 (updated for video, location and feedback) · App: TickSnap (com.mgudde.rbg.ticksnap) · Developer: rbg

TickSnap is an interval camera for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: your photos stay on your phone, there is no account and there are no ads.

Photos you take

Photos are saved in TickSnap's private storage on your phone. If you turn on Also copy to phone Gallery or tap To Gallery, copies are saved in your phone's Pictures folder. We never receive your photos. They leave your phone only when you share them yourself through Android's share menu, to the app or person you choose. Password-protected ZIP passwords are never stored by the app.

Camera

TickSnap uses the camera to show the preview and take photos, including in the background while an interval loop you started is running (with a notification).

Videos and the microphone

In VIDEO mode TickSnap records video with the camera and, if you leave the sound switch on, audio with the microphone. The app asks for microphone access the first time you record with sound. Recording happens only while you're recording a video, and videos are saved in TickSnap's private storage on your phone like photos. We never receive them. If the privacy filter is on, faces are hidden in the video as it's recorded, on your phone.

Location (optional)

If you turn on Save location in photos, TickSnap asks for location access and writes where each new photo or video was taken into the file itself (GPS tags), like your phone's camera does. The location is read on your phone and never sent to us. It stays inside the file, including when you share the original or copy it to your Gallery; photos shared as "Smaller" leave it out. The setting is off unless you turn it on, and you can switch it off at any time.

Feedback and ratings

Send feedback, Request a feature and Report a bug open your email app with a message to us; bug reports include your phone model, Android version and TickSnap version, which you can see and edit before sending. After a few launches TickSnap may show Google Play's own rating window once; Google Play handles it and doesn't tell us whether you rated.

Face and hand detection

TickSnap detects faces (to show where they are) and hands (so you can wave or hold up your palm to take a photo). This uses Google ML Kit and Google MediaPipe models that run entirely on your phone. Camera images are not sent to us or to Google for this.

Data collected by Google ML Kit

Google ML Kit, which TickSnap uses for face detection, sends Google technical information about how the feature performs. According to Google's ML Kit disclosure, this includes:

DataExamplesPurpose
Device informationManufacturer, model, Android versionDiagnostics and usage analytics

Performance and usage of the featureLatency, image format and size, feature events, error codesDiagnostics and usage analytics

Google encrypts this data in transit and says it does not transfer it to third parties. It does not include your photos or the faces detected in them. Google's handling is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the ML Kit terms.

Purchases (TickSnap Pro)

TickSnap Pro is an optional one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. The app asks Google Play on your phone whether this Google account owns TickSnap Pro, and remembers the answer on the phone so Pro keeps working offline. We don't run a server and don't receive your purchase details beyond what Google Play shows us in the Play Console (such as order numbers and country, for refunds and tax). Google's handling is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

App updates

When TickSnap starts, it asks the Google Play Store app on your phone whether a newer version is available. This check is handled by Google Play.

What we don't do

  • We don't run servers, collect analytics of our own, or show ads.
  • We don't access your contacts or other apps' files; the microphone only while you record a video with sound, and location only if you turn on Save location in photos.
  • We don't sell or share personal information.

Children

TickSnap is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete loops or photos inside the app, or uninstall TickSnap to remove everything it stored. Copies you saved to your Gallery or shared stay where you put them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
